🌙 About

Hello, beautiful people. I'm Ben, and this is the home of the Firekin, a kind, generous, slightly sassy little community that is very much on fire.

The streams are cosy, low input, and sensory friendly. No loud music, no sudden noises, just a warm space to settle into. Everyone is welcome here, and the channel is explicitly LGBTQIA+ welcoming and 18+.

Come lurk, come chat, come exist quietly in the corner. There is never any pressure to do anything but be yourself.
