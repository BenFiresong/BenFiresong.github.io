🌈 What we do

Every month the Firekin gather for a charity marathon, a longer cosy stream where everything we raise goes straight to a cause that matters to this community.

Past marathons have supported causes close to our hearts, including Rainbow Railroad, who help LGBTQIA+ people find safety from persecution.

There is never any pressure to give. Lurking, cheering, and spreading the word all help just as much. Every little bit is welcome, and so are you.
